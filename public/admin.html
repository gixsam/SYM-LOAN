Terms of Service & Policy Framework

SYM EMPIRE PLATFORM (S.E.P.) Financial Architecture

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or using SYM LOAN and the SYM EMPIRE PLATFORM (S.E.P.), you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to be legally bound by these Terms of Service.

2. Borrower Eligibility & Identity Requirements

All borrowers must be at least 18 years of age and legal citizens of Bangladesh. Valid Smart National ID (NID) registration, live biometric selfie verification, and mobile phone authentication via Telegram or SMS are strictly mandatory.

3. Repayment Schedule & Default Remedies

Loan principal and agreed fee schedules must be repaid on or before the due date. Failure to repay triggers automatic strike escalation. Borrowers reaching 3 strikes are permanently blacklisted across S.E.P. credit networks and subject to lawful recovery.

4. Amendments & Platform Authority

SYM EMPIRE PLATFORM reserves the exclusive right to modify limits, fees, and operational policies with notice via the client notifications desk.

1. Data Collection & Security

We collect your verified telephone number, Smart NID document scans, email, and live camera capture solely for fraud prevention, KYC verification, and transaction auditing. All data in transit and at rest is secured using industry-standard 256-bit encryption.

2. Non-Disclosure Commitment

Your personal documents, financial ledger history, and contact details will never be sold, rented, or shared with commercial marketing agencies.

3. Camera & Biometric Permissions

Camera access is utilized strictly for one-time live KYC selfie capture to verify physical ownership of the applicant identity, eliminating stolen ID fraud.

1. Loan Issuance & Disbursement

Approved funds are disbursed via secure Mobile Financial Services (bKash, Nagad, Rocket) or bank wire. Official digital payment vouchers are issued with unique cryptographically traceable transaction IDs.

2. Transparent Fees (Zero Hidden Charges)

All disbursement fees, interest or service charges are explicitly displayed prior to loan acceptance. S.E.P. guarantees 100% transparency with zero undisclosed surcharges.

3. Dispute Resolution & Legal Jurisdiction

Any legal claims, disputes, or recovery proceedings arising out of this agreement shall be governed under the laws and competent financial courts of Dhaka, Bangladesh.
